# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BenchGuard a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize to affected people, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing another person's private information, and conduct reasonably
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may issue a
private correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban based on impact
and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub's maintainers/contact controls. Do not open a public issue containing
sensitive personal information. Maintainers will protect the reporter's
privacy and disclose conflicts of interest. Reports about a maintainer will be
handled by another maintainer where possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available
at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
